# New Feature Ideas for Yaaro Coach (from competitor scan)

I looked at 7 coaching apps: Coachway, Trainerize, PT Distinction, Coach Catalyst, HubFit, Everfit, and WellnessZ. Here's what I found, in simple words.

---

## 1. Features almost every app has (the "must-haves")

These showed up in nearly all 7 apps, so clients/coaches expect them by default:

1. **Workout programming** – build training programs, assign to clients, track history.
2. **Nutrition / meal plans** – meal plans, macro targets, food logging (some use AI to build meals or scan food photos).
3. **In-app messaging** – chat between coach and client, often with voice notes, photos, video.
4. **Payments** – collect one-time or recurring payments from clients inside the app (Stripe is common).
5. **Check-ins / forms** – recurring forms (daily/weekly) clients fill out, coach reviews them in one inbox.
6. **Habit tracking** – simple daily habits (water, steps, sleep) clients tick off, coach sees streaks.
7. **Progress photos & body measurements** – clients upload photos, log weight/measurements over time.
8. **Scheduling / calendar** – book sessions/appointments with clients.
9. **Automation** – auto-send welcome messages, reminders, or content on a schedule instead of coach doing it manually.
10. **Branded mobile app** – coach's own logo/colors on the client-facing app (white-label).
11. **Lead capture / sales pipeline** – forms to catch new leads and move them from "new" to "won".
12. **Community / challenges** – group feed, leaderboards, fitness challenges to keep clients engaged.

---

## 2. What Yaaro Coach already has

Good news — the foundation is strong:

- Client list + detailed client profile (overview, calendar, notes, stats charts)
- Program editor & routine editor (build workouts, supersets etc.)
- Program library & exercise library
- Body measurements logging + charts
- Progress pictures tab
- Advanced stats / muscle group stats
- Workout history & active program tracking per client
- Dashboard (greeting, stat cards, weekly active clients chart, latest activity)
- "Grow" module — lead capture form + leads panel (this is the start of a sales pipeline)
- Basic settings (profile, preferences, notifications)
- Google login

So training/programming and client progress tracking are already solid — that matches what every competitor treats as "core."

---

## 3. What's missing — recommended features to build next

### High priority (every competitor has this, we don't)
- **Nutrition / meal plans** — no meal planning or macro tracking exists yet. This is one of the biggest gaps.
- **In-app messaging** — no chat between coach and client. Right now coaches have to use notes only, no back-and-forth.
- **Payments / billing** — no way to charge clients or manage packages/subscriptions inside the app.
- **Check-in forms** — no recurring check-in form system (only manual notes exist).
- **Habit tracking** — no simple daily habit checklist for clients.

### Medium priority (common, adds a lot of value)
- **Scheduling / booking calendar** — clients can book sessions with the coach (the current "calendar card" only shows activity, not booking).
- **Automation / reminders** — auto-send onboarding messages, check-in reminders, etc.
- **Branded client app / white-label** — let coaches put their own logo/colors for their clients.
- **Sales pipeline stages for leads** — the "Grow" leads panel could be extended into a proper pipeline (New → Contacted → Won), like Coachway/PT Distinction do.

### Nice to have (good for engagement, not urgent)
- **Community feed / challenges** — group posts, leaderboards, badges.
- **On-demand video library** — pre-recorded workout videos clients can follow anytime.
- **Wearable integrations** — connect Apple Health, Fitbit, Garmin, etc. for auto step/calorie data.
- **AI assistant** — auto-generate workout or meal plans, or content ideas (several competitors now advertise this as a headline feature).

---

## 4. Suggested next step

If I had to pick just 3 things to build next, in order of impact:
1. **In-app messaging** (clients expect to talk to their coach without leaving the app)
2. **Nutrition/meal plans** (pairs naturally with the existing workout programs)
3. **Check-in forms + habit tracking** (keeps clients engaged between sessions, and every competitor leans on this for retention)

Payments and branding can come after, once the core coaching loop (train + eat + check in + chat) is complete.
